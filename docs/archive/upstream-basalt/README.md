# Upstream Basalt history

granite is forked from [Basalt](https://github.com/jesdga95/basalt) (MIT). The files here are upstream's history up to the
fork, kept as they were: they describe Basalt, its models, its `.basalt` packs and its Blackwell cards, not granite.

- [Changelog](changelog/index.md): Basalt's releases, 0.1.0 to 0.5.6. Their links point into upstream's tree at each
  release; many of those pages are not in granite.
- [Community runs](community/benchmarks.json): the benchmark rows submitted to Basalt before the fork. granite's own rows
  start empty in [docs/community/benchmarks.json](../../community/benchmarks.json) ([community runs](../../community.md)).
